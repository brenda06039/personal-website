这些年，我一直在和“不会”打交道。 从企业培训到艺术教育，从项目管理到品牌运营，从对接政府、企业、高校，到自学PS、排版、视频剪辑以及新媒体运营，我的大部分技能都来自同一句话——“要不先试试？”

事实证明，很多事情并没有想象中那么难。人最有趣的地方在于，你永远不知道自己下一项技能会在哪里被点亮。

所以我喜欢变化，也习惯学习。每一次跨界、每一个新项目，都像打开一个新的盲盒。有人追求稳定，我更享受成长带来的惊喜。

直到今天，我依然相信学习是件很划算的事。它不一定让人立刻变厉害，却能让人面对变化时没那么慌张。毕竟人生最大的确定性，大概就是它一直在变化。

Over the years, I’ve been constantly dealing with “I don’t know how” moments.

From corporate training to art education, from project management to brand operations, from collaborating with governments, businesses, and universities, to teaching myself Photoshop, layout, video editing, and social media, most of my skills grew out of one simple sentence: “Why not just give it a try?”

It turns out that many things aren’t as difficult as they seem. The most interesting thing about people is that you never know where your next skill will be sparked.

That’s why I enjoy change and have made learning a habit. Every cross-industry leap, every new project, feels like opening a mystery box. While some people chase stability, I relish the surprises that growth brings.

Even today, I still believe learning is worth it. It may not make you instantly brilliant, but it makes facing change a lot less frightening. After all, the only certainty in life is that it keeps changing.
